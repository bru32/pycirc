CoilCirc Help

The fin and tube coil is made up of a bank of tubes packed into a stack of fins. The problem is, how do you link the tubes.

In the most simple case, you could link all the tubes into a single circuit. This is easy to do but not very practical. The problem is pressure drop! All of the tube side fluid would have to be forced through one little tube.

At the other extreme, you could link all tubes to headers at either end. This would give a low pressure drop but very bad for heat transfer.

The way to link the tubes is a compromise between maximum heat transfer and maximum pressure drop.
